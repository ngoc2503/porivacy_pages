Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

Thank you for using TinyLock: Touch Lock for Kids ("TinyLock", "we", "our", or "us").

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how TinyLock collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

TinyLock is designed to protect your device from accidental touches while watching videos.

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal details.

2. Permissions Used

TinyLock may request the following permissions:

  • Display Over Other Apps
    This permission allows TinyLock to display the floating bubble used to quickly enable or disable touch lock. The floating bubble works only on your device and is never used to collect personal information.
  • Accessibility Service
    TinyLock may use Android Accessibility Service to provide touch-lock functionality and improve the user experience. The Accessibility Service is used only for the features described in the app. TinyLock does not use Accessibility Service to collect, monitor, record, or transmit personal information.
  • Notifications (Optional)
    Notifications may be used to provide service status or quick actions.

3. PIN Code

If you create a PIN, it is stored locally on your device.

Your PIN is never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone.

4. Purchases

TinyLock offers optional premium features through Google Play Billing.

Payment information is processed securely by Google Play.

We do not receive or store your payment information.

5. Advertising

The free version of TinyLock may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google may collect certain device information according to its own Privacy Policy.

For more information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

TinyLock is intended to help parents prevent accidental touches while children watch videos.

The application is not designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that any child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address the issue.

7. Data Security

TinyLock stores only the minimum information necessary for its functionality.

Any locally stored settings remain on your device.

8. Third-Party Services
